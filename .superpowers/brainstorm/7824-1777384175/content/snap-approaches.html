A — Fixed Grid

The whole board has an invisible grid (e.g. every 40px). Cards always snap to the nearest grid point, wherever you drop them. Predictable and clean.

B — Align to Nearest Card

When you drop a card within range of another card, it snaps to share the same left edge, top edge, or row. No global grid — cards line up with each other.
