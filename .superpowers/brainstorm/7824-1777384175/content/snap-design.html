What's being built

  • While dragging a card, the app checks for other cards within 150px
  • If a nearby card is found → ghost appears aligned to its left or top edge
  • If no nearby card → ghost snaps to nearest 40px grid point
  • On release → card moves to where the ghost was
  • No extra UI to configure — fully automatic
